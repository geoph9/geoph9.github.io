I am a PhD student at the Centre for Speech Technology Research (CSTR) at the University of Edinburgh. My research focuses on the application of curriculum learning for unsupervised and lightly-supervised speech recognition models, and I am supervised by [Peter Bell](https://homepages.inf.ed.ac.uk/pbell1/) and [Ondrej Klejch](https://www.ondrejklejch.cz/).

Previously, I was a part of the Speech Recognition group at [Aalto University](https://research.aalto.fi/en/organisations/speech-recognition-2) in Helsinki, Finland, where I worked on curriculum learning for speech recognition with [Mikko Kurimo](https://research.aalto.fi/en/persons/mikko-kurimo) and [Tamás Grósz](https://research.aalto.fi/en/persons/tam%C3%A1s-gr%C3%B3sz).

Besides Speech Recognition, my interests include the development of local-only/privacy-preserving smart home systems. I am always open to collaborate on new topics, so feel free to reach out!
